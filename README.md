# MSRA Open Research

An open knowledge base of nutrition and health research for every species, especially the exotic pets that science tends to overlook. We believe every species and every condition deserves the same standard of care.

Built by [MSRA — Mystical Species Research Academy](https://msralab.com), an AI-powered deep research lab.

**Read it on the website:** [msralab.com/research](https://msralab.com/research/) · [research library](https://msralab.com/research/library/)

## What's here

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`notes/`](notes/) | Research notes: what we looked at, what we found, and where the evidence stops. In English and 中文. |
| [`library/`](library/) | [`studies.csv`](library/studies.csv): the published studies we track, with links to PubMed and to free full text where it exists. GitHub shows it as a searchable table. |

## How we work

- **Every claim has a source.** Each reference carries a PubMed ID or DOI. Where we find no evidence, we write "not found" rather than fill the gap.
- **We say where the evidence stops.** Which species was studied, and whether a study tested an ingredient or a finished product.
- **AI-powered, human-reviewed.** Our research system searches and cross-checks the peer-reviewed literature; our team reviews every note before it is published.
- **Corrections are public.** Every change to a note is kept in this repository's history.
